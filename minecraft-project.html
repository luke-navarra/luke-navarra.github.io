Overview & Objectives

Cybersecurity concepts are frequently taught through abstract theoretical lectures, which can present a high barrier to entry for learners. Code Quest leverages Minecraft's immersive sandbox environment to make technical security concepts engaging, practical, and intuitive.

Instead of simply reading about cryptographic principles, access controls, or network traversal, students encounter these topics as interactive puzzles and quests within a custom-designed game world.

  • [✓]
    Gamified Learning: Replaces dry slides with hands-on exploratory challenges inside a familiar 3D world.
  • [✓]
    Experiential Problem-Solving: Teaches threat identification, authentication mechanisms, and privilege concepts.
  • [✓]
    Evaluated Pedagogical Outcome: Designed and tested as part of my First Class Honours dissertation at Cardiff University.
